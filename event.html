The Event

A Global Gathering for Peace & Prosperity

The AMS Global Peace & Diplomacy Event is a flagship summit convened by the Foundation. It brings together a curated assembly of world leaders, cultural ambassadors, and industry pioneers to the heart of Kathmandu.

This gathering serves as a neutral platform for high-level dialogue, focusing on mountain sustainability, cross-cultural heritage, and humanitarian collaboration across the 55+ countries represented here today.
