Our Heritage

Rooted in Kathmandu, Reaching the World

The AMS Global Himalaya Foundation is an international not-for-profit organization headquartered in the historic city of Kathmandu, Nepal. Inspired by the resilience and majesty of the Himalayas, we serve as a global bridge for cultural diplomacy and humanitarian progress.

Our foundation operates on the belief that the unique positioning of the Himalayas — as the "Roof of the World" — provides the ultimate sanctuary for global leaders to convene and solve the most pressing challenges of our time.

Foundation Pillars

Vision, Mission & Positioning

Guiding our global impact through strategic action and a shared future.

👁️

Our Vision

To establish Nepal as a premier global convening hub for dialogue and action at the intersection of peace, climate resilience, and sustainable tourism.

🎯

Our Mission

To recognize and encourage individuals, organizations, and institutions making significant contributions to tourism, environmental conservation, and community development. We aim to promote responsible tourism practices that safeguard the Himalayas and uplift local communities.

🗺️

Strategic Positioning

This platform is strategically positioned as a soft-power diplomacy platform, a climate and sustainability dialogue hub, and a global tourism leadership forum.
